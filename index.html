Meet Stew

The person behind Stew Did It Renovations

I started Stew Did It Renovations because I enjoy taking an idea someone has for their home and helping turn it into something real. To me, home improvement is personal. You're trusting someone in your space, and I believe that trust should be met with respect, clear communication, and work you can feel good about.

Whether you want to freshen up a room with paint, install new flooring, update a bathroom or kitchen, or take care of another project around the house, I bring the same care and attention to every job — big or small.

I want you to feel comfortable asking questions, talking through ideas, and being part of the process. At the end of the day, the goal is simple: create a finished space that feels right for you and your home.
